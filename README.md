# Software-Design-document
Briefly summarize The Gaming Room client and their software requirements. Who was the client? 
The client for this software is the gaming room who wants us to implement the Draw It or Lose It 1980’s Game.
What type of software did they want you to design?
They wanted us to design a web application that would render Images to different web browsers.
What did you do particularly well in developing this documentation?
I believe I did the best recommendation part on which platform they should use for the server-side operating platform of Draw It or Lose It.
What about the process of working through a design document did you find helpful when developing the code?
Going through the process of doing a design sheet allows you to see the bigger picture of what the system must do. After having that done, it will make the software development process easier, as you just have to look at the design sheet as you are building it. Think of it as looking at a blueprint.
If you could choose one part of your work on these documents to revise, what would you pick? How would you improve it?
I would pick up the requirements area, and I would have added a high-speed Ethernet card to get faster internet speeds.
How did you interpret the user’s needs and implement them into your software design? Why is it so important to consider the user’s needs when designing?
How did you approach designing software? What techniques or strategies would you use in the future to analyze and design a similar software application?
The steps required for interpreting the user’s needs are: First, I had to watch how Draw It or Lose It was so I could understand what was being asked. In the description, I couldn’t deduce what it was, so I thought of the things that a development team would need to make it into software. This process is important because we want to UNDERSTAND what the customer is asking of us and reiterate it to them so that we understand perfectly what is being asked. For designing another design sheet, I would apply the same steps from a top-to-bottom approach.
